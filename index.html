Live demo

Try it right now.

This is the real app engine, running in your browser. Press COMPLETED, add your own topics and pick a difficulty to watch topics move from New to Medium to Old.

The demo resets when you refresh. The full app adds subjects, timers, notes, streaks, history and reminders.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Daily pick

One topic. Zero decisions.

Open the app and your topic is waiting. All your energy goes into learning, not organising.

Smart rotation

Nothing gets forgotten.

Fresh material keeps coming back until it sticks, while older topics keep returning so they never fade.

Difficulty

Hard, Medium or Easy.

Tell the app how tough a topic feels and it adapts how long that topic stays in focus.

Subjects

As many as you like.

Keep maths, languages, code and certifications completely separate, each with its own rhythm.

Timer

See your hours add up.

Time every session and watch your total study time grow.

Notes

Leave yourself a trail.

Pin a permanent note to a topic, and write "where I stopped" so tomorrow starts fast.

Progress

XP, levels and streaks.

Earn XP, level up, protect your streak and fill your history calendar with colour.

Make it yours

Reminders and six themes.

Get a gentle daily nudge at your time, then pick the colours that make you want to open it.

How it feels

Three queues. One steady habit.

New3

slots every cycle. Fresh topics get the most attention.

Medium2

slots every cycle. Topics that have started to stick.

Old2

slots every cycle. Known topics, kept alive for good.

Miss a few days? You pick up exactly where you left off. No guilt, no backlog, no angry red numbers.

Privacy

Your data is yours. Completely.

No account. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. ReWiseQuest works fully offline and everything you create stays on your own PC, in a folder you choose.
